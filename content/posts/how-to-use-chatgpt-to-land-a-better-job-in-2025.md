The job market has changed. Candidates who use AI tools in their job search are getting more interviews, better offers, and faster results.

This is your complete playbook.

---

## Step 1: Rebuild Your Resume with AI

Most resumes are weak because they list duties instead of achievements. Use this prompt in ChatGPT:

```
I'm a [job title] with [X years] of experience. Here are my responsibilities:
[paste your current bullet points]

Rewrite these as achievement-focused bullet points using the formula:
"[Action verb] + [what I did] + [measurable result]"
```

Run every single bullet point through this. The difference is night and day.

---

## Step 2: Write Tailored Cover Letters in 3 Minutes

Paste the job description and your resume, then use:

```
Based on this job description and my background, write a compelling 
3-paragraph cover letter that:
1. Opens with a specific hook about the company
2. Shows how my experience matches their exact needs
3. Closes with a confident call to action

Job Description: [paste]
My Background: [paste resume summary]
```

Customize the output — never send AI-generated text without personalizing it.

---

## Step 3: Prepare for Interviews

Ask ChatGPT to simulate a real interview:

```
Act as a tough interviewer for this role: [job title] at [company type].
Ask me the 10 most likely interview questions, one at a time.
After each of my answers, give me honest feedback and suggest improvements.
```

Practice until your answers feel natural and confident.
